Functions of Balochistan Public Service Commission

According to the Balochistan Public Service Commission Act, 1989 and the Balochistan Public Service Commission (Functions) Rules, 1995, The Chater of duties of the Commission Incllude:-

Conduct of Test, Examination and Interviews for initial recruitment to

A) The Civil Sevices of the Province in B-16 to B-21, except those specified in the schedule appended to these rules

B) Any Other Posts Which may be referred to the commission for recruitment by the Government

For the efficient and Smooth discharge of functions, the Balochistan Public Service Commission is performing its function with the following operational branches which are futher divided into the different sections wings:-

  • Recruitment Branch.
  • Examination Branch.
  • Result Branch.
  • Research Branch.
  • Regulation Branch.
  • Administration Branch.
  • Account Branch.
  • Secrecy Branch.
  • Infromation Techonolgy Branch.
  • Human Resource Branch.
