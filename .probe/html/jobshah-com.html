How to apply safely, in three steps

Most rejected applications fail on small details. Follow this order every time.

  1. 1

    Find the notice

    Browse a category or search by department, city or job title. Open the post and check the posting date so you know the notice is current.

  2. 2

    Check the details

    Read the eligibility, age limit, required documents and last date before you start. If you do not meet a condition, move on and save your time.

  3. 3

    Apply on the official channel

    Use the official link in the post and submit well before the deadline. Never pay an agent to submit a form on your behalf.

What Jobshah is, and what it is not

Jobshah.com collects recruitment advertisements from government departments, the armed forces, police, banks and private companies, then rewrites the important parts in plain language: who can apply, which documents are needed, the last date and where to apply. We link to the official source wherever one exists so you can verify everything yourself.

Jobshah is an information website. We do not hire, we do not collect application fees and we are not connected to the departments we write about. If a department changes a date or cancels a notice, we update the post.

Questions readers ask us

Is Jobshah.com an official government website?

No. We are an independent website that summarises public recruitment notices. For the final word, always open the official advertisement linked in the post.

Do I have to pay to apply through Jobshah?

No. Reading our posts is free and we never take money for applications. Some departments charge an official fee, which is printed in their advertisement and paid to them directly. Be careful with anyone who asks you to pay a middleman.

How do I know a job is still open?

Check the last date at the top of each post. Posts show an Open, Closing soon or Closed label when a last date is available.

I found a mistake in a post. What should I do?

Please write to us at Contact@Jobshah.com with the link to the post. We review every message and correct posts quickly.

Missing a notice or spotted an error?

Send us the details and we will review it for the site.
